  • schema.json: the record data model.

What to know before using it

  • Version 0.1, 21 October 2026. Ranks and grosses come from Wikipedia's annual tables, which draw on Box Office Mojo. They include later re-releases where the table does, and are still to be checked against Box Office Mojo.
  • The coding is provisional. Imaginary type and payoff were drafted with the assistance of an AI model and are under the editor's review. 6 films are marked not yet coded rather than guessed.
  • Circulation is not yet coded. State cooperation, script involvement and release columns are present but marked unknown; they will be filled only from sources.
  • "None found" is not "none". It records the state of the evidence, not a fact about the film.
