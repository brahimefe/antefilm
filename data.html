What to know before using it

  • Version 0.1, 21 October 2026. Ranks and grosses come from Wikipedia's annual tables, which draw on Box Office Mojo. They include later re-releases where the table does, and are still to be checked against Box Office Mojo.
  • The coding is provisional. Imaginary type and payoff were drafted with the assistance of an AI model and are under the editor's review. 6 films are marked not yet coded rather than guessed.
  • Circulation is not yet coded. State cooperation, script involvement and release columns are present but marked unknown; they will be filled only from sources.
  • "None found" is not "none". It records the state of the evidence, not a fact about the film.

Codebook

VariableValuesCoding rule
year2001–2025Year of worldwide release
rank1–10Position in that year's worldwide gross table
title, distributortextAs listed in the source table
grossUSDintegerWorldwide gross in US dollars, as listed
periodI–IVPeriod band: I 2001–2008, II 2009–2016, III 2017–2020, IV 2021–
imaginaryTypeintegrative, anomic-collapse, anomic-escape, retrotopian, restoration, personal, uncodedThe future the film's resolution offers; see the method, §8
payoffStructurepositive-sum, zero-sum, negative-sum, n/aWho gains in that future; n/a where the type is personal
codingNotetextThe coder's reason, where the coding is not obvious
recordNumber, recordURLtextThe full record, where one is filed
stateCooperationnone found, DoD, CIA, NASA, other US agency, foreign state, unknownAny state body that provided access, equipment, advice or script review; not yet coded
cooperationEvidencedocumented, reported, none foundDocumented: FOIA, official statement or credits; reported: credible secondary source
scriptInvolvementdocumented changes, advisory only, none found, unknownChanges requested or made at script stage by a cooperating body
releaseChina, releaseTurkeyyes, no, unknownTheatrical release in mainland China and in Turkey
