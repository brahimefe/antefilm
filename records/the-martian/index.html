The Martian2015, dir. Scott, number 10 worldwide that year

competent internationalism

The political imagination in which the camera record — a public space programme taken for granted, cooperation with China as common sense, competence as the remedy — meets a ground record that runs the other way: a Mars plan without a budget, a country without its own way to orbit, cooperation with China barred by law, and astronaut transport handed to private firms. One of the corpus's rare integrative futures, and on record it was not being built.

Record 007. Filed 21.10.2026. Ending coded integrative, positive-sum. Draft, under review.

The film shows what the film puts forward. The record shows what was enacted, on record, at the same time.

Construction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

The filmA storm strands the botanist Mark Watney alone on Mars. The problem is built as technical: food, water, distance, time. The Mars programme itself, its rockets, its budget and its schedule, is given.
The recordSix days after the film opened, NASA published its Journey to Mars plan, aiming to land humans on Mars in the 2030s. The plan named no missions, no timeline for landings and no cost. The programme the film takes as given was on record as a concept without a budget.
Sources and selection note
  1. NASA (2015). NASA's Journey to Mars: Pioneering Next Steps in Space Exploration. 8 October 2015.
  2. Davis, J. (2015). NASA's Journey to Mars report: a plan to form a strategy for a concept. The Planetary Society, 12 October 2015.

Selection note. This is a divergence record: the ground shows the imagination absent rather than enacted. Considered: NASA's 2015 budget request. Not filed: the plan speaks to the same construction directly.

Address

Who stands in for the species

The filmHumanity is the crowd in Times Square, Trafalgar Square and Beijing, watching one American's rescue on giant screens. The crew is international in spirit and American in command.
The recordIn the year of release, no American vehicle could carry astronauts to orbit. Since the shuttle's retirement in 2011 NASA had bought seats on Russian Soyuz rockets; in August 2015 it agreed to pay USD 490 million for six more, nearly USD 82 million a seat, after Congress underfunded the American replacement.
Sources and selection note
  1. CBS News (2015). NASA seats on Russian rockets will cost U.S. $490 million. 6 August 2015.

Selection note. Considered: NASA astronaut corps demographics. Not filed: dependence on Russia bears more directly on who could stand in for humanity in space in 2015.

Exclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

The filmThe rescue turns on cooperation. China's space agency offers its secret booster, and the American and Chinese programmes work as one. The film presents this as the obvious thing scientists would do.
The recordThe same word carries another file. Since April 2011 the Wolf Amendment, renewed in every appropriations act, has barred NASA from using its funds for any bilateral cooperation with China or Chinese-owned companies, or to host official Chinese visitors at its facilities.
Sources and selection note
  1. Department of Defense and Full-Year Continuing Appropriations Act, 2011, Public Law 112–10, section 1340 (the Wolf Amendment).
  2. Wikipedia (2026). Wolf Amendment, accessed 21 October 2026.

Selection note. Exclusion may range historically; the amendment was in force throughout the film's production and release. The statute itself should replace the encyclopedia source.

Prescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

The filmThe remedy is competence: public institutions, science and shared effort. Every crew member comes home, and the film closes on Watney teaching new astronauts as the next mission launches.
The recordPolicy prescribed a different remedy. In September 2014 NASA awarded up to USD 6.8 billion to two private companies, Boeing and SpaceX, to fly its astronauts. The public competence the film celebrates was being handed to the market.
Sources and selection note
  1. The Planetary Society (2014). NASA kicks off a private space race between Boeing and SpaceX. 17 September 2014.

Selection note. Divergence record. Considered: the 2015 NASA Authorization debates. Not filed: the contracts are the enacted remedy.

Circulation

The infrastructure that carried the film

The film
Production supportNASA worked closely with the production, according to the director; about 40 NASA staff attended a Washington screening.
Script involvementAdapted by Drew Goddard from Andy Weir's novel; NASA reviewed technical accuracy and called the film mostly accurate.
Distribution20th Century Fox worldwide release, including mainland China.
AuthorityNASA announced evidence of liquid water on Mars on 28 September 2015, days before release; NASA denied timing it to the film.
The record
On the same channelsUS security and science agencies worked on over 800 films and 1,000 television titles, according to records obtained under freedom-of-information requests.
Off the same channelsThe legal bar on US–China space cooperation has no comparable channel to the public.

  1. The New Daily (2015). NASA denies conspiring with Hollywood. 30 September 2015.
