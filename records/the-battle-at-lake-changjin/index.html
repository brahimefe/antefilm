The Battle at Lake Changjin2021, dir. Chen / Tsui / Lam, number 2 worldwide that year

martyr sovereignty

The political imagination in which the camera record — a nation encircled, soldiers as the nation, martyrs beyond question, sacrifice as security — coincides with the ground record: a centenary speech of steel walls, the dead flown home under fighter escort, a critic jailed for mocking the film's soldiers, and a rising defence budget. The future is secured by the dead, and doubting them is a crime.

Record 010. Filed 21.10.2026. Ending coded restoration, zero-sum. Draft, under review.

The film shows what the film puts forward. The record shows what was enacted, on record, at the same time.

Construction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

The filmIn 1950 American forces advance toward the Chinese border and bomb across it. The problem is built as encirclement: a superpower at the gate. China's entry into the war is given as defence.
The recordThree months before the film opened, at the Communist Party's centenary on 1 July 2021, the General Secretary warned that any foreign force trying to bully China would meet a Great Wall of steel forged by 1.4 billion people. The same construction, a nation encircled, was stated as policy.
Sources and selection note
  1. Reuters (2021). Xi warns against foreign bullying as China marks party centenary. 1 July 2021, as published by Cyprus Mail.

Selection note. Considered: China's 2019 defence white paper. Not filed: the centenary speech falls in the film's period and is the occasion the film was commissioned for.

Address

Who stands in for the species

The filmThe "we" is the Seventh Company and two brothers, Wu Qianli and Wu Wanli, who stand for a nation of soldiers. The dead are named and honoured; the Americans are seen from a distance.
The recordOn 2 September 2021, a month before release, an air force plane escorted by two fighters brought the remains of 109 Chinese People's Volunteers home from South Korea to Shenyang, the eighth such return since 2014, received in a state ceremony.
Sources and selection note
  1. The State Council of the PRC (2021). Remains of 109 Chinese soldiers killed in Korean War return. 3 September 2021.

Selection note. Considered: PLA recruitment drives in 2021. Not filed: the repatriation records who stands for the nation in the film's own terms.

Exclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

The filmThe keyword is martyr. The soldiers freeze and die without retreating, and the film asks the audience to honour them without question.
The recordThe same word carries another file. In October 2021 police detained the former journalist Luo Changping after he mocked the soldiers in a post about the film; in May 2022 a court in Sanya sentenced him to seven months for impugning the reputation of heroes and martyrs.
Sources and selection note
  1. Radio Free Asia (2022). Chinese journalist sentenced in Hainan for smearing reputation of CCP heroes. 6 May 2022.

Selection note. Exclusion may range historically; this record concerns the film itself. Considered: the 2018 Heroes and Martyrs Protection Law. Not filed alone: the Luo case shows the law applied to this film.

Prescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

The filmThe remedy is sacrifice now for security later: the soldiers fight so that the next generation will not have to. The film closes on the enemy's withdrawal.
The recordOn 5 March 2021 China announced a defence budget of 1.355 trillion yuan, about USD 209 billion, up 6.8 per cent on the previous year.
Sources and selection note
  1. Janes (2021). China announces 6.8% increase in 2021 defence budget. 5 March 2021.

Selection note. Considered: the 2021 conscription regulations. Not filed: the budget is the enacted scale of the remedy.

Circulation

The infrastructure that carried the film

The film
Production supportCommissioned by the Central Propaganda Department for the Party's centenary, with the National Radio and Television Administration and the Central Military Commission; budget about USD 200 million, the most expensive Chinese film made.
Script involvementDirected by Chen Kaige, Tsui Hark and Dante Lam under the commission.
DistributionOpened in China on 30 September 2021, before National Day; the highest-grossing Chinese film until 2025.
AuthorityPremiered as the opening film of the Beijing International Film Festival.
The record
On the same channelsA sequel, The Battle at Lake Changjin II, followed in 2022 and also entered the worldwide top ten.
Off the same channelsAccounts that the war began with North Korea's invasion of the South do not move through the same channels; questioning the film's soldiers was prosecuted.

  2. Radio Free Asia (2022). 6 May 2022.
