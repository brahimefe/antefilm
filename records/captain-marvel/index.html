Captain Marvel2019, dir. Boden / Fleck, number 5 worldwide that year

armed refuge

The political imagination in which the camera record — infiltrators revealed as refugees, a widened "we", a family reunited, a guardian sent into space — meets a ground record that kept only the last of these: refugees framed as terrorist entry, the lowest admissions ceiling on record, families separated at the border, and a new armed service for space. The film's integrative future travelled; the state enacted its armed half.

Record 008. Filed 21.10.2026. Ending coded integrative, positive-sum.

The film shows what the film puts forward. The record shows what was enacted, on record, at the same time.

Construction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

The filmThe Skrulls are built as shape-shifting infiltrators, enemies who can look like anyone. Halfway through, the film reveals them as refugees fleeing an empire's war of extermination.
The recordOn 27 January 2017 the President signed an order titled "Protecting the Nation from Foreign Terrorist Entry into the United States". It suspended refugee admissions for 120 days, stopped Syrian refugees indefinitely and cut the year's ceiling from 110,000 to 50,000. Refugees were constructed as possible infiltrators.
Sources and selection note
  1. Executive Order 13769, 27 January 2017. Summary in Wikipedia (2026), accessed 21 October 2026.

Selection note. Considered: the March 2017 revised order (13780). Not filed: the January order states the construction most directly. The Federal Register text should replace the encyclopedia source.

Address

Who stands in for the species

The filmThe film ends with its heroine adopting the refugees as her cause. The "we" widens from a US Air Force pilot to a displaced people with children.
The recordThe year the film was released, the United States admitted at most 30,000 refugees, and in September 2019 set the next year's ceiling at 18,000, the lowest since Congress created the resettlement programme in 1980.
Sources and selection note
  1. Pew Research Center (2019). Key facts about refugees to the U.S. 7 October 2019.

Selection note. Divergence record: the film widens the "we", the record narrows it. Considered: UNHCR's 2019 global resettlement figures. Not filed: the agent condition favours US policy.

Exclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

The filmThe emotional resolution is a family reunited: the Skrull commander finds his wife and daughter, hidden and alive.
The recordThe same word carries another file. In January 2019 the Inspector General of the Department of Health and Human Services reported that the government may have separated thousands more migrant children from their parents than the 2,737 already identified, and that poor records left the true number unknown.
Sources and selection note
  1. Al Jazeera (2019). US may have separated 'thousands more' migrant children: audit. 17 January 2019 (on HHS OIG report OEI-BL-18-00511).

Selection note. Exclusion may range historically; this record falls within the film's own period. The OIG report itself should be linked when finalised.

Prescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

The filmThe remedy is an empowered guardian in space. Carol Danvers leaves Earth to protect the refugees across the galaxy, and Nick Fury drafts a plan for a new defence initiative.
The recordOn 20 December 2019 the National Defense Authorization Act created the US Space Force, a sixth armed service within the Department of the Air Force — the service that had supported the film.
Sources and selection note
  1. Via Satellite (2019). Trump formally establishes Space Force. 23 December 2019.

Selection note. Considered: the 2018 National Defense Strategy. Not filed: the Space Force is the enacted remedy closest to the film's guardian in orbit.

Circulation

The infrastructure that carried the film

The film
Production supportUS Air Force access to airmen, installations and aircraft; the star flew in an F-16 and toured an F-15 at Nellis Air Force Base, hosted by Brig. Gen. Jeannie Leavitt, the service's first female fighter pilot.
Script involvementThe Air Force has advised Marvel productions on the depiction of its pilots since Iron Man (2008).
DistributionDisney worldwide release; USD 1.13B.
AuthorityThe Air Force said the film was not part of a recruiting strategy; the Air Force Academy later said it saw no unusual rise in women applicants after the film.
The record
On the same channelsUS security agencies worked on over 800 films and 1,000 television titles, according to records obtained under freedom-of-information requests.
Off the same channelsThe record of refugee admissions and family separation reached the public through inspectors general and courts, not through the studio channels that carried the film.
