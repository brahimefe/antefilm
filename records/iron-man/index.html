IConstruction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

Camera recordTony Stark, an arms manufacturer, is ambushed in Afghanistan by insurgents carrying his own company's weapons. The problem is built as leakage: weapons in the wrong hands. Arms production itself is left as given; the fault lies in where the weapons end up.
Ground recordA year before the film, the US Government Accountability Office found a discrepancy of at least 190,000 weapons between what the Pentagon reported issuing to Iraqi security forces and its own property records. Leakage was on record as a feature of US arming policy, not an accident of rogue dealers.

Sources

  1. US Government Accountability Office (2007). Stabilizing Iraq: DOD Cannot Ensure That U.S.-Funded Equipment Has Reached Iraqi Security Forces. GAO-07-711, 31 July 2007.

Selection note

Considered: US weapons transfers to Afghan forces. Not filed: no audit of equal standing for the period was found.

IIAddress

Who stands in for the species

Camera recordStark, Rhodes and Pepper Potts are the film's "we". Afghans appear as captors, as villagers of Gulmira to be rescued, and as Yinsen, the doctor who dies so that Stark can escape. The rescue of Afghans is performed by an American in a suit he built.
Ground recordIn 2008, the year of release, the UN mission in Afghanistan counted 2,118 civilian deaths, the highest since 2001. It attributed 828 of them, 39 per cent, to pro-government and international forces. The Afghans the record names were killed on both sides of the line the film draws.

Sources

  1. UN News (2009). Number of Afghan civilian deaths in 2008 highest since Taliban ouster, says UN (on UNAMA's annual report).

Selection note

Considered: Afghan refugee figures for 2008. Not filed: civilian deaths by attributed party bear directly on the film's division of Afghans into victims and captors.

IIIExclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

Camera recordStark flies to Gulmira alone and ends the insurgents' hold on the village. He ends the film by declaring "I am Iron Man." The keyword is the private hero: force abroad, used by an individual, answerable to no one.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 16 September 2007, Blackwater guards escorting a US embassy convoy in Baghdad's Nisour Square killed 17 Iraqi civilians. Four were convicted in 2014 and pardoned by the President in December 2020. Private force abroad, on record, was neither heroic nor answerable for long.

Sources

  1. Wikipedia (2026). Nisour Square massacre, accessed 21 October 2026 (trial and pardon dates).

Selection note

Exclusion may range historically. A court record or Department of Justice release should replace the encyclopedia source before the record is finalised.

IVPrescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

Camera recordThe remedy is the inventor. Stark shuts down weapons production and builds a suit that only he controls. A privately held technology, owned by one man, replaces the army and the state.
Ground recordThe same prescription was policy. In 2008 the Congressional Budget Office estimated at least 190,000 contractor personnel on US-funded contracts in the Iraq theatre, about one per US service member, a ratio at least 2.5 times higher than in any other major US conflict.

Sources

  1. Congressional Budget Office (2008). Contractors' Support of U.S. Operations in Iraq. Summarised in Contractors in Iraq, 12 August 2008.

Selection note

Considered: Pentagon procurement totals for 2008. Not filed: contractor numbers bear more directly on the substitution of private for public force.

VCirculation

The infrastructure that pre-clears the framing

Camera record
Production supportUS Air Force: three days of filming at Edwards Air Force Base, with an F-22 Raptor and a Global Hawk; about 150 airmen and a dozen Marines as paid extras.
Script involvementCapt. Christian Hodge served as the Defense Department project officer overseeing the Air Force's involvement.
Distribution channelsParamount worldwide release; Marvel Studios' first self-produced film.
Authority infrastructureOfficial Air Force news stories promoted the production and its airmen.
Ground record
On the same channelsUS security agencies worked on over 800 films and 1,000 television titles, according to entertainment-liaison records obtained under freedom-of-information requests.
Off the same channelsNo counter-distribution exists for the Afghan civilian casualty record through the same studio and liaison channels.

Sources

  1. Miles, D. (2007). Edwards team stars in 'Iron Man' superhero movie. American Forces Press Service, 2 May 2007.
  2. University of Bath (2017). US government influence on films and television. 28 June 2017.

privatised salvation

The political imagination in which the camera record — leakage as the problem, the inventor as the remedy, a private suit as the instrument of rescue abroad — coincides with the ground record: weapons unaccounted for by the state that supplied them, contractors in numbers equal to soldiers, and private force abroad that escaped account. The future is saved by one owner of one technology.
