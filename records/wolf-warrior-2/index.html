IConstruction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

Camera recordCivil war breaks out in an unnamed African country. Rebels and Western mercenaries threaten Chinese factory workers and African civilians alike. The chaos arrives as a local condition; the Chinese factory is simply there, a bystander to be protected.
Ground recordThe presence the film leaves as given is on record as a site of conflict. In 2011 Human Rights Watch documented poor safety, excessive hours, below-market pay and obstruction of unions at Chinese state-owned copper mines in Zambia.

Sources

  1. Human Rights Watch (2011). "You'll Be Fired if You Refuse": Labor Abuses in Zambia's Chinese State-owned Copper Mines. November 2011.

Selection note

Same-period condition: the report predates period III; filed provisionally, as no comparable institutional report of 2017–2020 has yet been found. Replace when one is.

IIAddress

Who stands in for the species

Camera recordLeng Feng, a discharged special-forces soldier, stands for the nation abroad. The "we" is the Chinese citizen: factory workers, a medical team, a shop owner. Africans appear as children to be saved and villagers who follow.
Ground recordThe citizen the film addresses had already been rescued at scale. In February 2011 China brought 35,860 of its citizens out of Libya by charter flights, ships and military transport, and a navy frigate, the Xuzhou, escorted evacuation ships — the navy's first evacuation abroad.

Sources

  1. GlobalSecurity.org (n.d.). China: Non-combatant evacuation operations (on the Libya evacuation, 2011).

Selection note

Considered: the 2015 Yemen evacuation. Not filed: the available figures do not reconcile.

IIIExclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

Camera recordLeng Feng fights beside African workers and calls them brothers. The keyword is protection: the Chinese presence shields the people around it.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 15 October 2010, at the Collum coal mine in Sinazongwe, Zambia, two Chinese managers shot 11 workers protesting conditions. No one was prosecuted; the charges were dropped and compensation negotiated.

Sources

  1. Human Rights Watch (2011). "You'll Be Fired if You Refuse". November 2011 (account of the Collum shooting).

Selection note

Exclusion may range historically. Collum is privately owned, not state-owned; the agent condition is met at the level of Chinese enterprise abroad, not the state.

IVPrescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

Camera recordThe remedy is the state's reach. A navy ship fires once authorisation arrives, and the film closes on a Chinese passport and a message to citizens: when you meet danger abroad, do not give up.
Ground recordOn 1 August 2017, days after the film's release, China formally opened its first overseas military base, in Djibouti, officially a logistics facility for ships on peacekeeping and humanitarian missions off Yemen and Somalia.

Sources

  1. Al Jazeera (2017). China opens first overseas base in Djibouti. 1 August 2017.

Selection note

Considered: the 2015 defence white paper on protecting overseas interests. Not filed yet: not opened for this record.

protective reach

The political imagination in which the camera record — a Chinese presence that only protects, a citizen the state will reach anywhere, a passport as the last word — coincides with the ground record: mass evacuations of citizens, an overseas base opened in the week of release, and a labour record the protection does not cover. Rescue exceptionalism, filed from the other side of the world.
