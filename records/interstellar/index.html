Interstellar2014, dir. Nolan, number 10 worldwide that year

exit imperialism

The political imagination in which the camera record — departure as evolutionary vocation, technological calling, humanitarian rescue — coincides with the ground record: departure as imperial operation, withdrawal as strategy, evacuation as doctrine. Film and policy travel as two registers of one imagination, not as text and context but as two operations of one configuration.

Record 001. Filed 16.05.2026, revised 21.10.2026. Ending coded anomic: escape, positive-sum.

The film shows what the film puts forward. The record shows what was enacted, on record, at the same time.

Construction

What is built as problem, what is left as given

The filmEarth is dying. Crops fail, dust takes the sky, an Iowa cornfield chokes. The cause is unnamed; the collapse arrives as condition, not consequence. The film inherits the dying world as premise and asks the audience to accept it without etiology.
The recordAn unnamed cause requires only exit. A named cause would require remedy by responsibility — extraction, war, climate policy. Months after the film opens, a Saudi-led air campaign begins in Yemen (March 2015), refuelled and supported with intelligence by the United States. By January 2016 the FAO counts 14.4 million Yemenis food insecure, and names the cause: conflict and import restrictions.
Sources and selection note
  1. FAO (2016). FAO warns of rapidly deteriorating food security in Yemen. Press release, 28 January 2016.
  2. Military Times (2015). U.S. launches aerial refueling mission in Yemen. 8 April 2015.

Selection note. Considered: drought-linked displacement in Syria, 2006–2010. Not filed: no US agent, and outside the film's period band.

Address

Who stands in for the species

The filmThe NASA compound assembles humanity's delegation: Cooper, Brand, Doyle, Romilly, Mann, Murph. The word humanity is spoken often. The bodies on screen are mostly white, mostly American, all addressed as universal.
The recordAt the end of 2014, 59.5 million people were forcibly displaced worldwide, 13.9 million of them newly displaced that year; Syria, Afghanistan and Somalia were the largest sources of refugees. None of these bodies were available to stand in for humanity. The film's universal subject is a regional one; the global record names the rest.
Sources and selection note
  1. UNHCR (2015). Global Trends 2014: World at War. Geneva: UNHCR. Release of 18 June 2015.

Selection note. Considered: US domestic poverty data, 2014. Not filed: it would keep the address national; the film's claim is to the species.

Exclusion

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

The filmTsiolkovsky cited as gospel: "Earth is the cradle of humanity, but one cannot live in a cradle forever." Departure as evolutionary vocation; the ground left behind never returns to the frame.01:12 — Brand quoting Tsiolkovsky in the launch sequence
The recordThe word departure carries another file: Saigon 1975, Mogadishu 1993, Kabul 2021 — departures producing ruin where they leave. At Kabul airport's Abbey Gate on 26 August 2021, during the evacuation, a suicide bombing killed at least 170 Afghans and 13 US service members. The framing requires these to remain structurally outside the same word.
Sources and selection note
  1. US Central Command investigation into the Abbey Gate attack, released 4 February 2022, as reported by Euronews (5 February 2022).

Selection note. Exclusion may range historically. Considered: the 1979 Tehran embassy evacuation. Not filed: Kabul 2021 is better documented and closes the film's period of US withdrawals.

Prescription

What the framing proposes as future-reality

The filmThe remedy is exit. Not repair, not redistribution, not return. The film closes with Cooper preparing for the next launch — departure is permanent vocation, not last resort.
The recordThe same prescription at policy level: withdrawal-as-strategy, evacuation-as-doctrine, departure-as-legitimating-modality of US imperial practice 2001–2021. Film and policy share one grammar of exit.
Sources and selection note
  1. Harvey, D. (2003). The New Imperialism. Oxford: Oxford University Press.

Selection note. Interpretive pairing; a documentary source for withdrawal doctrine (e.g. a strategy document of the period) would strengthen it.

Circulation

The infrastructure that carried the film

The film
Production supportNASA cleared archival footage and rights; it did not advise on the science.
Script involvementKip Thorne as scientific adviser and executive producer on physics, relativity and the depiction of black holes, from script through post-production.
DistributionParamount + Warner Bros. co-distribution; worldwide release.
AuthorityThorne's Caltech standing transferred to the film; its black-hole rendering was published as a peer-reviewed physics paper (James et al. 2015) and accompanied by Thorne's companion book.
The record
On the same channelsUS security agencies worked on over 800 films and 1,000 television titles, according to entertainment-liaison records obtained under freedom-of-information requests.
Off the same channelsNo counter-distribution exists for the operational record of US departures over the same period through the same studio infrastructure.
