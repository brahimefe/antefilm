What is built as problem, what is left as given

Transformers 2007machine alliance

Camera recordThe film opens on a US special-operations base in Qatar destroyed by an alien machine. The attackers are not human, so the war needs no cause. The American presence in the Gulf is given: the base is simply there.
Ground recordThe presence was on record. Since 2003 Qatar's Al Udeid Air Base has hosted the forward headquarters of US Central Command, with flights to Afghanistan and Iraq; by 2017 about 10,000 US troops were stationed there.

Iron Man 2008privatised salvation

Camera recordTony Stark, an arms manufacturer, is ambushed in Afghanistan by insurgents carrying his own company's weapons. The problem is built as leakage: weapons in the wrong hands. Arms production itself is left as given; the fault lies in where the weapons end up.
Ground recordA year before the film, the US Government Accountability Office found a discrepancy of at least 190,000 weapons between what the Pentagon reported issuing to Iraqi security forces and its own property records. Leakage was on record as a feature of US arming policy, not an accident of rogue dealers.

Interstellar 2014exit imperialism

Camera recordEarth is dying. Crops fail, dust takes the sky, an Iowa cornfield chokes. The cause is unnamed; the collapse arrives as condition, not consequence. The film inherits the dying world as premise and asks the audience to accept it without etiology.
Ground recordAn unnamed cause requires only exit. A named cause would require remedy by responsibility — extraction, war, climate policy. Months after the film opens, a Saudi-led air campaign begins in Yemen (March 2015), refuelled and supported with intelligence by the United States. By January 2016 the FAO counts 14.4 million Yemenis food insecure, and names the cause: conflict and import restrictions.

The Martian 2015competent internationalism

Camera recordA storm strands the botanist Mark Watney alone on Mars. The problem is built as technical: food, water, distance, time. The Mars programme itself, its rockets, its budget and its schedule, is given.
Ground recordSix days after the film opened, NASA published its Journey to Mars plan, aiming to land humans on Mars in the 2030s. The plan named no missions, no timeline for landings and no cost. The programme the film takes as given was on record as a concept without a budget.

Wolf Warrior 2 2017protective reach

Camera recordCivil war breaks out in an unnamed African country. Rebels and Western mercenaries threaten Chinese factory workers and African civilians alike. The chaos arrives as a local condition; the Chinese factory is simply there, a bystander to be protected.
Ground recordThe presence the film leaves as given is on record as a site of conflict. In 2011 Human Rights Watch documented poor safety, excessive hours, below-market pay and obstruction of unions at Chinese state-owned copper mines in Zambia.

Captain Marvel 2019armed refuge

Camera recordThe Skrulls are built as shape-shifting infiltrators, enemies who can look like anyone. Halfway through, the film reveals them as refugees fleeing an empire's war of extermination.
Ground recordOn 27 January 2017 the President signed an order titled "Protecting the Nation from Foreign Terrorist Entry into the United States". It suspended refugee admissions for 120 days, stopped Syrian refugees indefinitely and cut the year's ceiling from 110,000 to 50,000. Refugees were constructed as possible infiltrators.

The Battle at Lake Changjin 2021martyr sovereignty

Camera recordIn 1950 American forces advance toward the Chinese border and bomb across it. The problem is built as encirclement: a superpower at the gate. China's entry into the war is given as defence.
Ground recordThree months before the film opened, at the Communist Party's centenary on 1 July 2021, the General Secretary warned that any foreign force trying to bully China would meet a Great Wall of steel forged by 1.4 billion people. The same construction, a nation encircled, was stated as policy.

Top Gun: Maverick 2022preemptive nostalgia

Camera recordAn unnamed state is about to bring a uranium enrichment plant online, buried in a mountain bowl and ringed by surface-to-air missiles. The enemy has no name, no flag, no face. The problem is built as a deadline; the strike that answers it is given, never argued.
Ground recordOn 25 March 2025 the US Director of National Intelligence told the Senate that the intelligence community "continues to assess that Iran is not building a nuclear weapon." Three months later the President said Iran was "very close" and that the intelligence community was wrong. The deadline was built at the level of policy, against the government's own assessment.

Who stands in for the species

Transformers 2007machine alliance

Camera recordTwo "we"s carry the film: a teenage boy in an American suburb, and a squad of US soldiers who survive the desert attack and fight the machines at home.
Ground recordThe soldiers stood for a force at its largest. As of 1 November 2007, during the surge, 164,424 US troops were in Iraq, nearly 20,000 of them from the National Guard and Reserves.

Iron Man 2008privatised salvation

Camera recordStark, Rhodes and Pepper Potts are the film's "we". Afghans appear as captors, as villagers of Gulmira to be rescued, and as Yinsen, the doctor who dies so that Stark can escape. The rescue of Afghans is performed by an American in a suit he built.
Ground recordIn 2008, the year of release, the UN mission in Afghanistan counted 2,118 civilian deaths, the highest since 2001. It attributed 828 of them, 39 per cent, to pro-government and international forces. The Afghans the record names were killed on both sides of the line the film draws.

Interstellar 2014exit imperialism

Camera recordThe NASA compound assembles humanity's delegation: Cooper, Brand, Doyle, Romilly, Mann, Murph. The word humanity is spoken often. The bodies on screen are mostly white, mostly American, all addressed as universal.
Ground recordAt the end of 2014, 59.5 million people were forcibly displaced worldwide, 13.9 million of them newly displaced that year; Syria, Afghanistan and Somalia were the largest sources of refugees. None of these bodies were available to stand in for humanity. The film's universal subject is a regional one; the global record names the rest.

The Martian 2015competent internationalism

Camera recordHumanity is the crowd in Times Square, Trafalgar Square and Beijing, watching one American's rescue on giant screens. The crew is international in spirit and American in command.
Ground recordIn the year of release, no American vehicle could carry astronauts to orbit. Since the shuttle's retirement in 2011 NASA had bought seats on Russian Soyuz rockets; in August 2015 it agreed to pay USD 490 million for six more, nearly USD 82 million a seat, after Congress underfunded the American replacement.

Wolf Warrior 2 2017protective reach

Camera recordLeng Feng, a discharged special-forces soldier, stands for the nation abroad. The "we" is the Chinese citizen: factory workers, a medical team, a shop owner. Africans appear as children to be saved and villagers who follow.
Ground recordThe citizen the film addresses had already been rescued at scale. In February 2011 China brought 35,860 of its citizens out of Libya by charter flights, ships and military transport, and a navy frigate, the Xuzhou, escorted evacuation ships — the navy's first evacuation abroad.

Captain Marvel 2019armed refuge

Camera recordThe film ends with its heroine adopting the refugees as her cause. The "we" widens from a US Air Force pilot to a displaced people with children.
Ground recordThe year the film was released, the United States admitted at most 30,000 refugees, and in September 2019 set the next year's ceiling at 18,000, the lowest since Congress created the resettlement programme in 1980.

The Battle at Lake Changjin 2021martyr sovereignty

Camera recordThe "we" is the Seventh Company and two brothers, Wu Qianli and Wu Wanli, who stand for a nation of soldiers. The dead are named and honoured; the Americans are seen from a distance.
Ground recordOn 2 September 2021, a month before release, an air force plane escorted by two fighters brought the remains of 109 Chinese People's Volunteers home from South Korea to Shenyang, the eighth such return since 2014, received in a state ceremony.

Top Gun: Maverick 2022preemptive nostalgia

Camera recordThe Dagger squad — Rooster, Hangman, Phoenix, Bob, Payback, Fanboy, Coyote — is young, mixed in gender and background, and trained by a man of the previous generation. The "we" is the cockpit, the squadron, the carrier. The enemy pilots never lift their visors.
Ground recordThe audience the film hails was hailed directly. At screenings in May 2022, viewers reported Navy recruiting booths near the concession stands and the Air Force ran a recruiting advertisement before the film, while the Navy sought 40,000 enlisted sailors and 3,800 officers for fiscal year 2022.

Which carriers of the same keyword stay off-record

Transformers 2007machine alliance

Camera recordA secret agency, Sector Seven, has hidden alien technology for decades, answers to no one, and turns out to be on the right side. Secrecy is played for laughs and then for victory.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 17 December 2005 the President acknowledged that he had authorised the National Security Agency to intercept communications after 2001 without court approval, and said he had reauthorised the programme more than 30 times.

Iron Man 2008privatised salvation

Camera recordStark flies to Gulmira alone and ends the insurgents' hold on the village. He ends the film by declaring "I am Iron Man." The keyword is the private hero: force abroad, used by an individual, answerable to no one.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 16 September 2007, Blackwater guards escorting a US embassy convoy in Baghdad's Nisour Square killed 17 Iraqi civilians. Four were convicted in 2014 and pardoned by the President in December 2020. Private force abroad, on record, was neither heroic nor answerable for long.

Interstellar 2014exit imperialism

Camera recordTsiolkovsky cited as gospel: "Earth is the cradle of humanity, but one cannot live in a cradle forever." Departure as evolutionary vocation; the ground left behind never returns to the frame.
Ground recordThe word departure carries another file: Saigon 1975, Mogadishu 1993, Kabul 2021 — departures producing ruin where they leave. At Kabul airport's Abbey Gate on 26 August 2021, during the evacuation, a suicide bombing killed at least 170 Afghans and 13 US service members. The framing requires these to remain structurally outside the same word.

The Martian 2015competent internationalism

Camera recordThe rescue turns on cooperation. China's space agency offers its secret booster, and the American and Chinese programmes work as one. The film presents this as the obvious thing scientists would do.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. Since April 2011 the Wolf Amendment, renewed in every appropriations act, has barred NASA from using its funds for any bilateral cooperation with China or Chinese-owned companies, or to host official Chinese visitors at its facilities.

Wolf Warrior 2 2017protective reach

Camera recordLeng Feng fights beside African workers and calls them brothers. The keyword is protection: the Chinese presence shields the people around it.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 15 October 2010, at the Collum coal mine in Sinazongwe, Zambia, two Chinese managers shot 11 workers protesting conditions. No one was prosecuted; the charges were dropped and compensation negotiated.

Captain Marvel 2019armed refuge

Camera recordThe emotional resolution is a family reunited: the Skrull commander finds his wife and daughter, hidden and alive.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. In January 2019 the Inspector General of the Department of Health and Human Services reported that the government may have separated thousands more migrant children from their parents than the 2,737 already identified, and that poor records left the true number unknown.

The Battle at Lake Changjin 2021martyr sovereignty

Camera recordThe keyword is martyr. The soldiers freeze and die without retreating, and the film asks the audience to honour them without question.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. In October 2021 police detained the former journalist Luo Changping after he mocked the soldiers in a post about the film; in May 2022 a court in Sanya sentenced him to seven months for impugning the reputation of heroes and martyrs.

Top Gun: Maverick 2022preemptive nostalgia

Camera recordAdmiral Cain tells Maverick that drones will replace him: "The future is coming, and you're not in it." The film answers through the pilot. The keyword is judgement — a human in the cockpit who sees, decides and is accountable.
Ground recordThe same word carries another file. On 29 August 2021 a US drone strike in Kabul, aimed at a suspected bomber, killed 10 civilians, seven of them children. The head of US Central Command called it a mistake. Remote judgement, unaccountable at the point of decision, is the war the film leaves out.

What the framing proposes as future-reality

Transformers 2007machine alliance

Camera recordThe remedy is an alliance of soldiers and machines. Good robots fight beside US troops, F-22s and an Osprey; at the end the Autobots' leader invites more machines to come to Earth.
Ground recordThe same prescription was the Army's plan. In March 2007 the Government Accountability Office reviewed the Future Combat System, a network of manned and unmanned ground and air vehicles costed by the Army at about USD 160 billion and independently at up to USD 234 billion, and found most of its technologies immature.

Iron Man 2008privatised salvation

Camera recordThe remedy is the inventor. Stark shuts down weapons production and builds a suit that only he controls. A privately held technology, owned by one man, replaces the army and the state.
Ground recordThe same prescription was policy. In 2008 the Congressional Budget Office estimated at least 190,000 contractor personnel on US-funded contracts in the Iraq theatre, about one per US service member, a ratio at least 2.5 times higher than in any other major US conflict.

Interstellar 2014exit imperialism

Camera recordThe remedy is exit. Not repair, not redistribution, not return. The film closes with Cooper preparing for the next launch — departure is permanent vocation, not last resort.
Ground recordThe same prescription at policy level: withdrawal-as-strategy, evacuation-as-doctrine, departure-as-legitimating-modality of US imperial practice 2001–2021. Film and policy share one grammar of exit.

The Martian 2015competent internationalism

Camera recordThe remedy is competence: public institutions, science and shared effort. Every crew member comes home, and the film closes on Watney teaching new astronauts as the next mission launches.
Ground recordPolicy prescribed a different remedy. In September 2014 NASA awarded up to USD 6.8 billion to two private companies, Boeing and SpaceX, to fly its astronauts. The public competence the film celebrates was being handed to the market.

Wolf Warrior 2 2017protective reach

Camera recordThe remedy is the state's reach. A navy ship fires once authorisation arrives, and the film closes on a Chinese passport and a message to citizens: when you meet danger abroad, do not give up.
Ground recordOn 1 August 2017, days after the film's release, China formally opened its first overseas military base, in Djibouti, officially a logistics facility for ships on peacekeeping and humanitarian missions off Yemen and Somalia.

Captain Marvel 2019armed refuge

Camera recordThe remedy is an empowered guardian in space. Carol Danvers leaves Earth to protect the refugees across the galaxy, and Nick Fury drafts a plan for a new defence initiative.
Ground recordOn 20 December 2019 the National Defense Authorization Act created the US Space Force, a sixth armed service within the Department of the Air Force — the service that had supported the film.

The Battle at Lake Changjin 2021martyr sovereignty

Camera recordThe remedy is sacrifice now for security later: the soldiers fight so that the next generation will not have to. The film closes on the enemy's withdrawal.
Ground recordOn 5 March 2021 China announced a defence budget of 1.355 trillion yuan, about USD 209 billion, up 6.8 per cent on the previous year.

Top Gun: Maverick 2022preemptive nostalgia

Camera recordThe remedy is the crewed aircraft and the veteran. The strike succeeds by hand-flown skill, and Maverick escapes in a museum-era F-14 taken from the enemy's own hangar. The future is won by returning to the past's machines.
Ground recordOn 21 June 2025, seven US B-2 bombers dropped fourteen GBU-57 penetrators on the Fordow and Natanz enrichment sites, the first operational use of the weapon, while a submarine fired Tomahawks at Isfahan. The film's strike on a mountain enrichment plant was carried out, by crewed aircraft, three years after release.

Transformers 2007machine alliance

The political imagination in which the camera record — a Gulf base that needs no explanation, the deployed soldier as "us", a secret agency that is secretly good, machines as allies — coincides with the ground record: CENTCOM's forward base in Qatar, the surge's peak, warrantless surveillance, and a USD 160 billion bet on robotic war. The present order is restored, with better machines.

Restoration, Zero-sum

Iron Man 2008privatised salvation

The political imagination in which the camera record — leakage as the problem, the inventor as the remedy, a private suit as the instrument of rescue abroad — coincides with the ground record: weapons unaccounted for by the state that supplied them, contractors in numbers equal to soldiers, and private force abroad that escaped account. The future is saved by one owner of one technology.

Anomic: escape, Zero-sum

Interstellar 2014exit imperialism

The political imagination in which the camera record — departure as evolutionary vocation, technological calling, humanitarian rescue — coincides with the ground record: departure as imperial operation, withdrawal as strategy, evacuation as doctrine. Film and policy travel as two registers of one imagination, not as text and context but as two operations of one configuration.

Anomic: escape, Positive-sum

The Martian 2015competent internationalism

The political imagination in which the camera record — a public space programme taken for granted, cooperation with China as common sense, competence as the remedy — meets a ground record that runs the other way: a Mars plan without a budget, a country without its own way to orbit, cooperation with China barred by law, and astronaut transport handed to private firms. One of the corpus's rare integrative futures, and on record it was not being built.

Integrative, Positive-sum

Wolf Warrior 2 2017protective reach

The political imagination in which the camera record — a Chinese presence that only protects, a citizen the state will reach anywhere, a passport as the last word — coincides with the ground record: mass evacuations of citizens, an overseas base opened in the week of release, and a labour record the protection does not cover. Rescue as national reach, filed from outside Hollywood.

Restoration, Zero-sum

Captain Marvel 2019armed refuge

The political imagination in which the camera record — infiltrators revealed as refugees, a widened "we", a family reunited, a guardian sent into space — meets a ground record that kept only the last of these: refugees framed as terrorist entry, the lowest admissions ceiling on record, families separated at the border, and a new armed service for space. The film's integrative future travelled; the state enacted its armed half.

Integrative, Positive-sum

The Battle at Lake Changjin 2021martyr sovereignty

The political imagination in which the camera record — a nation encircled, soldiers as the nation, martyrs beyond question, sacrifice as security — coincides with the ground record: a centenary speech of steel walls, the dead flown home under fighter escort, a critic jailed for mocking the film's soldiers, and a rising defence budget. The future is secured by the dead, and doubting them is a crime.

Restoration, Zero-sum

Top Gun: Maverick 2022preemptive nostalgia

The political imagination in which the camera record — an unnamed nuclear deadline, a strike that needs no argument, the human pilot over the drone, the old machine over the new — coincides with the ground record: a deadline built against the state's own intelligence, a crewed strike on mountain enrichment sites, and a drone war whose errors stay off the screen. The future is secured by preempting it with the past.
